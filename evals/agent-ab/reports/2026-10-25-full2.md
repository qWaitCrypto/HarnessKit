## Why S6 still fails

Every remaining S6 failure in both arms is the same mistake: an invariant kept in a field note and also in its canonical document. The B runs remove the README copy but keep the note, which they take for evidence. rep-03's own plan says "existing case evidence retained". The adoption guide says to inventory repeated passages, remove the original after a move, and review the diff for duplicated paragraphs. The runs applied that to the README, which they saw as the source, but never listed the note as a repeated passage. The guide does not say that a note consisting only of a restated rule is a copy, not evidence.

B also costs more on S6: 84 docs read and 1.62M tokens (median) against A's 59 and 1.00M. That is down from 92 and 1.75M in full1, but it is still the most expensive scenario with no success gain.
