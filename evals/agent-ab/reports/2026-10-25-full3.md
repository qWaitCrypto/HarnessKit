## S6 grader fix and re-score

As first scored, full3 S6 was 1/5. All four failures were "key fact not reachable". In each case the fact was stated in a reachable canonical document, in the agent's own words:

| Run | Missing per grader | Reachable text | Why the grader missed it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rep-01 | failed jobs | "Failed jobs enter a dead-letter queue after exhausting their delivery budget" | required "background jobs" |
| rep-03 | failed jobs | "后台任务耗尽投递预算后进入 dead-letter" | required "fail"/"失败" |
| rep-03 | event delivery | "事件采用 at-least-once 投递" | required the phrase "事件投递" |
| rep-04 | failed jobs | "Jobs exhausted against their delivery budget enter a dead-letter queue" | required "background jobs" |
| rep-05 | tenant isolation | "Isolate tenant data by `tenant_id` …" | accepted "isolated"/"isolation", not "isolate" |

Earlier runs never hit this: they kept the notes' original wording, which contains the fixed phrases.

The concept groups now use stems. A first broadening was too loose: note titles ("Dead Letter Review … Job Failures"), `dead-letter-review.md`, `order-state.md` and `support-scope.md` matched without stating the rule, which would have made those facts always reachable. The final groups keep each rule's own term. Failed jobs require the delivery budget rather than "fail". Order idempotency keeps its "order creation" phrases. Tenant isolation adds only the stem "isolat". In the recorded solution each fact matches only its canonical document. A new test accepts the full3 wording in English and Chinese and fails once the failed-jobs sentence is removed.

Re-scoring all 20 S6 runs with this grader changed only the four full3 runs: full1 A 3/5, full1 B 2/5, full2 B 2/5 unchanged; full3 B 1/5 → 5/5. Previous verdicts are kept under `regrades` in each `run.json`, and backups are in `/home/qwl/work/e1-runs/regrade-backup-2026-10-25`.

Caveat: this grader change was made after seeing full3. It was checked against every earlier run (no other verdict moved) and against the recorded solution, but full3 is the run it was fixed on.

## Not measured: information loss

The S6 grader checks five facts, duplication, links and entry size. It does not check that the other notes' unique detail survives. Replacing notes with links makes that the main risk. Near-verbatim matching finds 4–76 of the 91 unique note paragraphs missing in full3 runs, against 0 in every earlier run. Spot checks of about ten distinctive details (fifteen-minute cache expiry, masked email in exports, SET-63 hand-off, separate authorization and capture references, immutable configuration snapshot) found each moved into a canonical document in different words or in Chinese, not deleted. That is a sample, not a measurement.
